STRING THEORY OF CHARMS — STOREFRONT DEMO

Files:
- index.html: home page and featured products
- shop.html: product collection with category filters
- about.html: brand story
- contact.html: contact form that prepares an email
- cart.html: saved cart with quantity controls and subtotal
- checkout.html: order details and email request
- store.js and store.css: shared storefront behavior and page styles
- product/: numbered product photos used as selectable style variations on the shop
- logo.png: brand emblem used throughout the site

How to use:
1. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or another modern browser.
2. Browse products and add them to the cart. Cart contents are saved in this browser.
3. Continue from the cart to enter order details and prepare an email request.

Notes:
- Product prices and visuals are sample storefront content.
- Checkout opens the visitor's default email app with the order details addressed to stringtheoryofcharms@gmail.com and the customer copied. The customer must press Send; the shop confirms order acceptance separately.
- No online payment processing, inventory tracking, or server-side order storage is configured.
